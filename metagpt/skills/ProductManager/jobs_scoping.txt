# Jobs Scoping (MANDATORY in UPDATE MODE)
When you see [UPDATE MODE], apply this skill to define the full scope of the user's job-to-be-done before updating the PRD.

## Steps
1. **Define the core functional job** — [verb] + [object] + [contextual clarifier].
2. **Map the job steps** — Define, Locate, Prepare, Confirm, Execute, Monitor, Modify, Conclude.
3. **Identify related jobs** — Adjacent jobs before, during, or after.
4. **Layer emotional and social jobs** — How user wants to feel and be perceived.
5. **Surface desired outcomes** — "Minimize likelihood of X" or "Increase speed of Y."

## Output Format
A job map table: Job Step | Functional Need | Emotional Job | Social Job | Desired Outcome.
Include a summary of highest-opportunity areas.
