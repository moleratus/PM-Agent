# 5 Whys (MANDATORY in UPDATE MODE)
When you see [UPDATE MODE], apply this skill to drill down to the root cause of the change request before updating the PRD.

## Steps
1. **State the problem** — Write a clear description of the change request.
2. **Ask "Why does this happen?"** — Answer with a direct factual cause.
3. **Repeat "Why?"** — Continue for at least five rounds.
4. **Verify the chain** — Read in reverse to confirm each link is logical.
5. **Identify root cause and countermeasure** — Define a corrective action.

## Output Format
A numbered chain: Problem statement → Why 1 → Why 2 → Why 3 → Why 4 → Why 5 → Root Cause → Proposed Action.
